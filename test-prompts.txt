5. Good prompts for testing your budget/replan streaming

These are particularly useful for testing the status messages you just implemented.

I want to visit Dubai for 5 days with a $500 budget. I prefer a 3-star hotel and I'm interested in sightseeing, food, and culture.

Expected flow should be something like:

Parsing your request...
Applying travel preferences...
Finding best flights...
Finding best hotels...
Finding best activities...
Planning your trip...
Checking budget...
Budget exceeded...
Replanning your trip...
Checking budget...
Replanning your trip...
Trip planned
Plan a 5-day trip to London with a $500 budget. I want a 3-star hotel and I'm interested in sightseeing and culture.

This should strongly exercise your budget exceeded → replan path because London hotels are expensive in your mock data.
